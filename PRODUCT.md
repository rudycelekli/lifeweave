# LifeWeave

register: product

LifeWeave is a local-first, proactive intelligent UI toolkit and personal workspace. It connects chosen ambitions to measurable goals, consented evidence and small, reviewable next actions. The product serves a person balancing career, health, relationships, learning, finances, home, contribution and enjoyment. The person chooses the dimensions and their importance; no universal life score is imposed.

## The experience

Open Today and see the most relevant next moves with their evidence and a useful working interface. Inspect why a move appeared, adjust the underlying goal, snooze it, dismiss it or mark the suggestion handled. Completing a suggestion is not proof of completing a goal. Useful surfaces include a focus planner, commitment review, metric trend, relationship reminder, decision comparison and weekly reflection.

The first console ships with clearly marked synthetic sample data. A separate personal workspace starts empty. Historical ambitions from supplied documents are optional candidates for review, never silently current goals. Current active goals require a local choice.

## Product boundaries

Observation, inference, proposal, authorization, execution and verified outcome are separate records. The first version observes local imports and synthetic fixtures, proposes declarative surfaces and records local feedback. It never sends mail, alters calendars, controls a desktop or infers a medical diagnosis. Provider adapters are read-only and require host-supplied access and explicit scopes. A connector catalog is not an account connection.

## Success

The initial build must run without a model account, replay realistic synthetic events, survive restarts, explain every recommendation, reject stale/foreign/revoked evidence and display unknown measurements honestly. It must offer a portable package API, a browser renderer, host adapters and a private console. The longer-term success measure is user-rated usefulness and independently observed progress with less oversight, acceptable interruption burden and no unauthorized actions. This version cannot establish that it improves life outcomes or is the world's best.
